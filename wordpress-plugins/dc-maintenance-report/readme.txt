Auto-generate polished, white-label monthly maintenance reports for your WordPress clients in seconds.

== Description ==

**DC Maintenance Report** uses GPT-4o to generate professional monthly maintenance reports from simple stats you enter — plugins updated, backups completed, uptime, security scans. The result is a premium, white-labeled client report that justifies your care plan pricing.

= What it does =
* Accepts monthly maintenance stats (updates, backups, uptime, security scans)
* Generates a full branded report: executive summary, health score, work completed table, value summary
* White-label with your agency name
* Copy to clipboard and paste into email or PDF in 60 seconds

= How to monetize =
* Justifies your **$99–$299/mo care plan** with a tangible deliverable
* Takes 60 seconds — **replaces 2+ hours of manual work**
* Clients who receive reports **churn significantly less**
* Use as a **free sample** to convert one-off clients to retainer

== Installation ==

1. Upload `dc-maintenance-report` to `/wp-content/plugins/`
2. Activate the plugin
3. Go to **Maint. Report → Settings** — enter your OpenAI API key and agency name
4. Fill in the monthly stats and generate your first report

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Can I white-label the reports? =
Yes. Your agency name is set in Settings and appears in every generated report.

= Can I save past reports? =
Version 1.0.0 generates reports on demand. Copy to clipboard and paste into your preferred tool (email, Notion, PDF generator).
